# 赛场招募口播 / On-site Recruiting Script

## 15 秒版本 / 15-second Version

中文：

我想做 SentryFlow AI，把 eufy 摄像头变成海外远程能源场站的 AI 巡检员。先做太阳能 / 储能场站的夜间入侵、设备区异常和每日巡检报告。这个场景有付费价值：远程场站分散、人工巡检贵、传统 CCTV 只录像不解释。我有 10 年 IT 和实时数据处理经验，想找 2-3 位队友补多模态、前端 Demo、IoT 或海外市场调研。

English:

I want to build SentryFlow AI, turning eufy cameras into AI inspection agents for overseas remote energy sites. The MVP starts with night intrusion, equipment-zone anomalies, and daily inspection reports for solar and energy storage sites. The scenario has paid value: distributed sites, expensive manual inspection, and CCTV that records without explaining risk. I have 10 years of IT and real-time data processing experience, and I am looking for 2-3 teammates for multimodal AI, frontend demo, IoT, or overseas market research.

## 30 秒版本 / 30-second Version

中文：

我这个项目叫 SentryFlow AI，想把 eufy 摄像头从“运动提醒”升级成“AI 巡检员”。主场景是海外太阳能 / 储能场站，因为这类场站位置分散、夜间无人值守、人工巡检成本高，而且传统 CCTV 只能录像，不能告诉你“发生了什么、风险多大、下一步该怎么处理”。

MVP：夜里有人进围栏，或者设备区出现遮挡、低光、烟雾火光、异常停留，系统识别风险，生成解释和处置建议，在 Dashboard 里展示，并自动形成每日巡检报告。它不是只做识别，而是做“巡检工作流”。

我本人有 10 年 IT 经验，可以承担全栈和大数据实时处理，熟悉日志采集、事件处理、过滤、监控、告警。AI 方向我在学 Agent、RAG、Text-to-SQL、平台 API 编排和实时计算 + AI 推理。现在想找 2-3 位队友，最好能补多模态 / CV、前端 Demo、eufy SDK / IoT，或者海外调研和 Pitch。

English:

This project is called SentryFlow AI. The idea is to upgrade eufy cameras from motion alerts into AI inspection agents. The primary scenario is overseas solar and energy storage sites, because these sites are distributed, often unattended at night, expensive to inspect manually, and traditional CCTV cannot tell operators what happened, how serious it is, or what to do next.

MVP: when someone enters a fenced area at night, or when the equipment-zone view is blocked, too dark, shows smoke / fire, or has abnormal stay, the system detects the risk, explains it, recommends actions, displays it in a dashboard, and generates a daily inspection report. The focus is the whole inspection workflow.

I have 10 years of IT experience and can cover full-stack development and real-time big data processing, including log collection, event processing, filtering, monitoring, and alerting. On the AI side, I am learning Agents, RAG, Text-to-SQL, platform API orchestration, and real-time computing with AI inference. I am looking for 2-3 teammates for multimodal / CV, frontend demo, eufy SDK / IoT, or overseas research and pitch.

## 适合问对方的问题 / Questions to Ask Potential Teammates

- 你更想做 AI、多模态、前端、硬件接入，还是市场 Pitch？ / Are you more interested in AI, multimodal, frontend, hardware integration, or market pitch?
- 你有没有做过摄像头、视频分析、IoT 或 Dashboard？ / Have you worked on cameras, video analysis, IoT, or dashboards?
- 你愿不愿意做一个场景很具体、Demo 可以快速跑通、商业故事也比较清楚的项目？ / Would you like to work on a project with a specific scenario, fast demo potential, and a clear business story?

## 可以强调的一句话 / Key Line to Emphasize

中文：

这个项目不是只做一个 AI 识别 Demo，而是把摄像头事件、AI 判断、处置建议和巡检报告串成一个完整闭环。

English:

The project connects camera events, AI judgment, recommended actions, and inspection reports into a complete workflow.
