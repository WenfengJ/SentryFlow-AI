# SentryFlow AI 黑客松方案 / Hackathon Brief

## 一句话 / One-liner

SentryFlow AI 让 eufy 摄像头成为远程太阳能与储能场站的 AI 巡检员。  
SentryFlow AI turns eufy cameras into AI inspection agents for remote solar and energy storage sites.

## 问题 / Problem

远程能源场站很难持续巡检。业主和运维团队需要知道：是否有人进入禁区、摄像头是否被遮挡、是否出现烟雾或火光、夜间是否存在未处理风险。  
Remote energy sites are difficult to inspect continuously. Owners and operators need to know whether someone entered a restricted area, whether a camera view is blocked, whether smoke or fire appears, and whether unresolved risks exist after hours.

传统 CCTV 只记录视频，但不解释风险，也不会自动总结运营状态。  
Traditional CCTV records video, but it does not explain risk or summarize operational status.

## 方案 / Solution

SentryFlow AI 监听摄像头事件和视频画面，并通过 AI Agent 生成风险标签、场景解释、处置建议和每日巡检报告。  
SentryFlow AI monitors camera events and video frames, then uses an AI Agent to produce risk labels, scene explanations, recommended actions, and daily inspection reports.

## 主场景 / Primary Scenario

海外远程太阳能与储能场站。  
Remote solar and energy storage sites in overseas markets.

## 扩展场景 / Expansion Scenarios

- 工业园区 / Industrial parks
- 工厂 / Factories
- 矿区 / Mines
- 仓库 / Warehouses
- 远程工地 / Remote construction sites

## MVP / MVP

黑客松 MVP 聚焦三个流程：  
The hackathon MVP focuses on three workflows:

1. 夜间入侵检测 / Night intrusion detection
2. 设备区异常检测 / Equipment-zone anomaly detection
3. 每日巡检报告生成 / Daily inspection report generation

## 差异化 / Differentiation

- 聚焦远程工业和能源资产，而不是通用家庭安防 / Focused on remote industrial and energy assets, not generic home security
- 输出 Agent 风格解释，而不仅是目标检测结果 / Agent-style explanation instead of only object detection
- 面向运维团队生成每日报告 / Daily report output for operations teams
- 与 eufy 摄像头硬件能力和海外渠道存在潜在结合点 / Potential fit with eufy camera hardware capabilities and overseas distribution

## Demo 叙事 / Demo Narrative

一个海外太阳能场站在围栏和设备区安装了多台 eufy 摄像头。夜间，系统检测到有人进入禁区。它不只是发送运动提醒，而是解释风险、建议处置动作，并把事件写入下一份每日巡检报告。  
An overseas solar site has several eufy cameras installed around the perimeter and equipment area. During the night, the system detects a person entering the restricted zone. Instead of only sending a motion alert, SentryFlow AI explains the risk, recommends actions, and includes the event in the next daily inspection report.
