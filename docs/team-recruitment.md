English:

I want to build an Anker / eufy hackathon project called **SentryFlow AI**. In one sentence: **turn eufy cameras into AI inspection agents for overseas remote energy sites**.

I want to start with a specific scenario that is easy to explain and has real business value: overseas solar and energy storage sites. Many of these sites are distributed, unattended at night, expensive to inspect manually, and traditional CCTV only records footage without explaining risk. SentryFlow AI is meant to detect night intrusion, obstruction, low light, smoke / fire, or abnormal stay near equipment, then explain what happened, why it matters, what to do next, and summarize it in a daily inspection report.

MVP: **camera footage / events enter the system -> AI judges the risk -> explanation and action are generated -> dashboard displays the event -> daily inspection report is created**. Judges can see a complete flow, and the same idea can later expand to factories, industrial parks, mines, warehouses, and remote construction sites.

My strength is execution. I have 10 years of IT experience and can cover full-stack development plus real-time big data processing, including log collection, event processing, filtering, monitoring, and alerting. On the AI side, I am learning Agents, RAG, Text-to-SQL, platform API orchestration, and real-time computing with AI inference. I can own the camera event flow, AI analysis pipeline, report generation, and demo integration, while also supporting overseas research and market analysis.

I am looking for 2-3 teammates for multimodal / CV, frontend demo, eufy SDK / IoT integration, or market pitch. If you are interested in **hardware + AI + real-world scenarios + commercialization potential**, let us talk.

## MVP 可以怎么演示 / MVP Demo

1. 夜间入侵：识别有人进入围栏 / 设备区，并解释为什么是高风险 / Night intrusion: detect a person entering a fenced or equipment area and explain why it is high risk

3. 风险解释：输出“发生了什么、为什么危险、建议怎么处理” / Risk explanation: output what happened, why it matters, and what action to take
4. 事件工作台：展示风险等级、摄像头位置、截图、处理状态 / Event workbench: show risk level, camera location, snapshot, and review status
5. 每日巡检报告：自动总结事件次数、设备状态、未处理事项 / Daily inspection report: summarize event count, device status, and unresolved issues
